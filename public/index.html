How these numbers are calculated

Training & embodied footprint — the bigger picture

A single query is only the inference (running) part — running the model to answer you. "Embodied" means the footprint of building it: manufacturing the chips and servers it runs on, plus training the model itself. That one-time build cost is real but is spread across billions of queries, so running (inference) usually still adds up to more overall.

Linear scale shows absolute bar lengths; smaller values may be difficult to see beside training emissions. Exact values are shown on each bar.

Web search vs AI — which tool for which job?

A single search is a fraction of a single chat query's energy, but both are tiny. The right choice balances task, accuracy and footprint. For everyday search, Ecosia is the more sustainable option: it runs on 100% renewable energy and produces ~2x the renewable energy its searches use. Synthesis — where it's all heading

Macro context — AI and electricity

Data-centre demand is projected to roughly double by 2030, with AI driving a disproportionate share. Projections from IEA 2025 and 2026 research preprints.

Water — the undercounted resource

Water follows energy: each kWh of data-centre electricity embeds ~5.3 L of total water use (direct cooling + indirect power-plant water). The derived line below scales the electricity projection by that WUE; the benchmark shows a larger UNU-INWEH estimate.

Australian context: data centres & AI infrastructure

Australia has ~162 operational data centres and one of the world's largest construction pipelines. Federal expectations for developers (March 2026) are guidance, not law.

Australia's data-centre build-out

Operational capacity is forecast to grow from ~1.4 GW to ~3.2 GW by 2030 (Data Centres Australia / DC Byte). The much larger announced numbers below are not directly comparable to operational capacity — they are aspirational pipelines and connection queues, and "phantom demand" means many applications never get built.

Method & caveats

No public study measures a single end-user chatbot query end-to-end (provider data is proprietary). Per-query energy here is derived from published per-token / per-inference measurements (2025–2026 papers) combined with your inputs: prefill and decode energy per token, PUE overhead, Australian grid carbon intensity (~500 g CO2e/kWh, IEA) and API list pricing. Water is derived from energy using the total data-centre WUE of ~5.3 L/kWh (1.9 L/kWh direct cooling, EESI; 3.4 L/kWh indirect power-plant water, Patterns 2026). Figures are mid-range assumptions — real values vary by hardware, model version, batching, context length and provider location. CO2 depends heavily on grid mix; water depends on cooling technology and region. Treat results as order-of-magnitude estimates.

Sources are grouped by trust level, from peer-reviewed papers (most weight) down to vendor self-reports (treated as promotional).
