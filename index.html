Method & caveats

No public study measures a single end-user chatbot query end-to-end (provider data is proprietary). Per-query energy here is derived from published per-token / per-inference measurements (2025–2026 papers) combined with your inputs: prefill and decode energy per token, PUE overhead, Australian grid carbon intensity (~500 g CO2e/kWh, IEA) and API list pricing. Water is derived from energy using the total data-centre WUE of ~5.3 L/kWh (1.9 L/kWh direct cooling, EESI; 3.4 L/kWh indirect power-plant water, Patterns 2026), plus a fixed per-provider baseline. Figures are mid-range assumptions — real values vary by hardware, model version, batching, context length and provider location. CO2 depends heavily on grid mix; water depends on cooling technology and region. Treat results as order-of-magnitude estimates.

Sources are grouped by trust level, from peer-reviewed papers (most weight) down to vendor self-reports (treated as promotional).
